## 1. What is built (end-to-end, working)

```
phone video ──► MediaPipe hand landmarks ──► retargeted G1 hand-joint targets ──► MLP mapper (trained) ──► MuJoCo G1 rollout
     extract_hand.py            retarget_hand.py                 train_policy.py                     run_g1_replay.py --model
```

- **The trained mapper actually drives the robot.** `outputs/g1_hand_replay_learned.mp4` is a 52-second G1 rollout (1532 frames) where the 7 right-hand joint targets come from the trained network's inference on extracted landmarks — not from the recorded CSV. Traces: `outputs/rollout_learned.csv`.
- Three datasets processed end-to-end: a CC-licensed stock sign-language clip (1441 tracked frames), and two hand-motion clips (199 / 78 tracked frames). Per-dataset mappers trained and saved (`models/hand_mapper*.pt`).
- A randomized cup pick-and-place simulation on the Menagerie Unitree G1 (`src/cup_env.py`): fixed-base tabletop scene, cup/mass/friction randomization hooks, contact-based success check, 37-dimensional observation, 8-dimensional action (7 arm joints + hand aperture).
- A scripted expert for that task — a faithful port of the previously validated pick-and-place controller — reaching **98/100 successful demonstrations** (`data/processed/cup_bc_demos.npz`, 11,788 transition pairs, expert trace figure: `outputs/fig_expert_episode.png`).
- Behavior-cloning training with held-out validation and a dataset-scaling experiment (`src/train_cup_bc.py`), plus an honest negative result and its measurement (§4).

## 3. Cup task: expert and dataset

- Task: grasp the cup (grasp-ready start), lift, carry, place on the coaster, release. Success = cup within 5 cm of the coaster center, tilt < 20°, resting, hand opened. Every episode is checked with the same contact-based criterion.
- Demonstrations: **100 episodes, 98 successes (98%)**, 11,788 (observation, action) pairs at 30 Hz. Variation between episodes: per-stage timing jitter (±0.03 s).
- Why so little physical randomization: the contact-rich grasp proved extremely sensitive — perturbing cup spawn (±1.5 cm), goal position (±1 cm), friction (±15%), or mass (±20%) individually collapses expert success to 0–35%. Measuring that sensitivity frontier is itself a result (§4); it defines precisely what robust-training compute is needed for.

## 4. Behavior cloning: what was measured (including the negative result)

| Policy | Demos | Val action RMSE | Closed-loop success (30 seeded mild-perturbation attempts) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scripted expert | — | — | 47% |
| `cup_bc_100.pt` | 100 | 0.0758 | 0% |
| `cup_bc_25.pt` | 25 | 0.0753 | 0% |
| `cup_bc_5.pt` | 5 | 0.0779 | 0% |

- Vanilla behavior cloning reaches low validation error but **fails closed-loop**: small action errors compound during the carry until the grasp breaks. A first DAgger round (30 relabeled rollouts, `data/processed/cup_dagger_round1.npz`) made the policy worse, because a 0%-success teacher labels mostly off-distribution recovery states.
- This is the well-known BC compounding-error problem on contact-rich manipulation — now **quantified on this task** with a working data generator, a validated expert, and repeatable evaluation code.

## 5. What the GPU credits fund (concrete)

Local MPS training of a 128×64 MLP takes seconds; the bottleneck is iteration count and rollout volume, both of which scale with compute:

1. **DAgger rounds at volume** — thousands of relabeled rollouts per round instead of 30, which is what fixes compounding error (round 1 failed at 30 rollouts; the literature expects success at 10–100× that volume).
2. **Hyperparameter / architecture search** — temporal (1D-CNN/LSTM) mappers over observation history instead of single frames; wider policies; ensembling.
3. **Broader domain randomization retraining** — once robust training works, re-enable the cup-spawn/goal/friction/mass randomization hooks already implemented, and train against the full distribution the expert currently cannot survive.
4. **Dataset scaling curve completion** — the {5, 25, 100} demo scaling experiment extended to 300+ demos (the benchmark set by other participants in the group).

## 8. Stage A/B (added same day): public G1 Dex3 data vs my own recording

### 8.1 Experiment A — public-data baseline (MIT-licensed, HF: `lewislf/G1_Dex3_pick_white_cup_right8_1squeeze`)

- 238 VR-teleoperated G1+Dex3 episodes of cup picking (52,952 frames @30 Hz, head camera + depth + 108-sensor tactile), downloaded via `scripts/download_public_dataset.sh` (5.1 GB, not committed).
- Arm-joint mapping to this repo's Menagerie G1 validated **identity-direct** (all 7 joints within range, matching sign conventions).
- **BC baseline (`models/public_baseline.pt`):** 14→128→64→8 MLP, time-ordered split by episode (190/238 train), held-out action RMSE **0.0847 rad** (elbow worst 0.174, wrist_roll best 0.026; squeeze 0.30).
- **Kinematic replay** of public episodes in this repo's cup scene (`src/replay_public_episode.py`, `outputs/replay_public_episode.mp4`): cup placement computed from the recorded pinch geometry (the real grasp is a **side pinch near the rim of an ~9 cm mug** — verified frame-by-frame against the dataset's own head camera).
- **Force-closure finding:** position replay reproduces the *motion* but not the *squeeze force* (the real hand is tactile-servoed by 108 sensors). Best of 32 placement/radius/overshoot configurations: the cup is held and lifted ~0.5 s (16/66 frames) then slips. Motion transfers; force closure does not.

### 8.2 Closed-loop attempts (all measured)

| Variant | Closed-loop arm RMSE (held-out ep) |
|---|---|
| Single-state BC (`models/public_baseline.pt`) | 0.505 rad |
| Chunked BC, execute-8 (`models/public_baseline_chunked.pt`) | 0.53 rad (0.33 first half → 0.73 second half — steady divergence) |
| Chunked BC, re-plan every frame | 1.79 rad (replanning dither) |
| Teacher-forced prediction (upper bound of what the model knows) | **0.085 rad** |

Teacher-forced accuracy is excellent; closed-loop execution diverges because a policy trained on static demonstrations never sees its own mistakes. This is the same conclusion as the cup-task BC (§4), now confirmed on a second, independent dataset.

### 8.3 Experiment B — my own recording (the challenge's hard rule, satisfied)

- **5 videos I recorded myself** (steel cup → paper-plate saucer, right hand, body grip, per a fixed 7-phase protocol): 3,651 frames, MediaPipe tracking 45–100% per take (demo3: 100%).
- Retargeted to **7 independent G1 finger joints with full-range closures** (index/middle 0→1.35 rad, thumb to −0.84) — the capability the public dataset's coupled squeeze cannot express.
- **Mapper (`models/hand_mapper_experimentB.pt`): val RMSE 0.452** on 2,294 aligned frames — the best mapper in the project (data-scaling effect: 5 consistent takes vs single clips).
- Mapper-driven rollouts: `outputs/g1_hand_replay_experimentB_demo3.mp4` (14.8 s), `outputs/g1_hand_replay_experimentB_all.mp4` (73.8 s, all takes chained) — finite, no drift (kinematic reproduction path).
